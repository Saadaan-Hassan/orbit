# Support

Orbit is a solo-maintainer, beta-stage project. There is **no SLA** on any
of the channels below — response times are best-effort.

## Getting help

- **Usage questions, install problems, "is this a bug?"** — open a
  [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Saadaan-Hassan/orbit/issues) using the
  Question/Support template.
- **Something's broken** — open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/Saadaan-Hassan/orbit/issues/new/choose).
  Include your Orbit version, macOS version, and steps to reproduce.
- **You want a feature** — open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/Saadaan-Hassan/orbit/issues/new/choose).
  No promise it gets built, but it's the right place to propose it.
- **Privacy question, or something feels off about what's captured** — use
  the Privacy Concern issue template, or email saadaanedu@gmail.com if you'd
  rather not make it public.
- **Security vulnerability** — do **not** use a public issue. Follow
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## What isn't in-scope here

- General macOS or Tauri/Rust/FastAPI troubleshooting unrelated to Orbit
  itself — try the relevant upstream project's own support channels first.
- Feature requests for capabilities explicitly listed as future/unbuilt
  phases in `AGENTS.md` (Windows support, voice, screenshots) — these are
  already tracked; a duplicate issue won't speed them up.

## Before asking

Check the [README](README.md)'s Known Limitations section and the
[open-source readiness roadmap](OPEN_SOURCE_ROADMAP.md) — a fair number of
"is this expected?" questions are already answered there.
